Equipment ↔ WO Subject Relationship

A WO's subject can be a piece of customer equipment — a boiler, a heat pump, a chiller. Many-to-many: a WO can carry multiple subject equipment items, and an equipment item exposes its full WO history (powering the QR-scan-to-history loop in UIS-15E).

Status: Concept Effort: ~3d Extends: WO-1 · UIS-15E Master ref: #20

New components: <SubjectEquipmentPicker />, <EquipmentServiceHistoryTab />
API surface: GraphQL — new join model WorkOrderSubjectEquipment (workOrderId, equipmentId, role?, attachedAt, attachedByUserId); standard getList signatures on both sides

Two distinct equipment relationships per the master plan: SUBJECT (the AC unit being serviced) vs USED_WITH (the lift truck used to perform the work, already part of WO-2 line items). This spec covers SUBJECT only — many-to-many because a single visit may service multiple HVAC units in the same building.

3. Key decisions for Emma + Corey to discuss

D1 Single subject or many subjects per WO?
Recommended: Many — Emma's call. Service techs frequently work on 2-3 RTUs in a single visit; forcing separate WOs per unit kills throughput. Many-to-many WorkOrderSubjectEquipment join.
D2 Must subject equipment belong to the WO's customer?
Recommended: Yes, validated server-side. Picker only shows that customer's equipment; mutation rejects mismatched customerId. Prevents accidentally attaching another customer's RTU to a WO.
D3 Link to UIS-15E equipment QR?
Recommended: Yes — scanning an equipment QR on-site offers "Attach to active WO" as the primary action. Closes the field-to-history loop. Same mutation, just a different entry point.
D4 Carry the subject through to bills / invoices as a reference?
Recommended: Yes — optional subjectEquipmentId reference on invoice line items so the customer's invoice can read "Service performed on Carrier 50TC-008 RTU." Hugely valued by service-co accounts.
D5 Allow the same equipment as both SUBJECT and USED_WITH on the same WO?
Recommended: Yes — they're independent relationships. Edge case: a customer's lift truck is being serviced (subject) and being used as a platform during the work (used-with). Rare but legitimate; no constraint needed.

4. Roadmap context
