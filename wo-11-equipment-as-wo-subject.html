WO-11 Work Orders P1 - 1.33 Draft

Equipment as Work Order Subject

Introduce a first-class relationship between a Work Order and the piece of equipment the work is being performed on. Surfaces every WO ever performed on a piece of equipment in a new "Work Orders" tab on the equipment detail page (desktop & mobile) — the foundation of a per-asset service history.

1. Business Case

Service-oriented contractors (HVAC, mechanical, refrigeration) deliver work that is fundamentally about a piece of equipment — a rooftop unit, a vending machine, a boiler. Each service visit produces a work order describing what was inspected, what was repaired, what materials were used, and how long it took. Over time, the equipment accumulates a service history that is critical to both the technician (who needs to see what the last person did) and the office (who reports on service frequency, cost, and warranty exposure per asset).

Today, Appello can attach equipment to a work order only via line items — equipment used with the work (forklift, scissor lift, etc.) for billing. There is no first-class concept of the equipment the work is being performed on. Without that relationship, technicians arriving on site cannot see prior work history, and the office cannot report service history per asset.

"We need to delineate between equipment that the work is being done on. Imagine a vending machine you have to go maintain. You're creating a work order, maybe by scanning the QR code on the side of the vending machine. The work order is associated with the equipment. We still have the add-equipment-line-items thing in case we have to charge out the equipment we used." — Corey Shelson, 2026-05-05 product call

2. Scope

Already Built

  • DONE Equipment model with QR code generation (UIS-15E)
  • DONE Job model with jobClassification on PropertyValue distinguishing Work Orders from project jobs (WO-1)
  • DONE WorkOrderEquipmentItem — equipment line items used with the WO (WO-2)
  • DONE EquipmentTimeSheetRecordValue — structured equipment time entries on WOs (EQ-0)
  • DONE Equipment detail page (desktop & mobile) — adding a new tab is a UI extension

In Scope (WO-11)

  • IN Optional FK subjectEquipmentId on Job with relation to Equipment
  • IN "Equipment Serviced" field on the WO create / edit form (desktop & mobile) — searchable equipment combobox, optional
  • IN Subject equipment displayed on the WO detail page header (desktop & mobile)
  • IN "Work Orders" tab on the Equipment detail page (desktop & mobile) — chronological list of WOs where this equipment is the subject, with status, completion date, technician, and link through to each WO
  • IN "Subject Equipment" column on the Work Orders list page (filterable; sortable)

Out of Scope

  • OUT In-app QR scanning — equipment QR codes already exist (UIS-15E); the native phone camera handles the scan
  • FUTURE "Scan equipment QR → auto-create WO with this equipment as subject" deep-link flow
  • FUTURE Linking subject-equipment WOs to MaintenanceSchedule — covered by sibling spec WO-12 (Maintenance ↔ Work Order Linkage)
  • FUTURE Subject equipment for non-WO job types — surfaced only on WOs in V1
  • OUT Many-to-many subject equipment per WO — explicitly rejected (see "Decided" callout below for reasoning)
  • FUTURE Polymorphic subject (e.g., a Location, a vehicle as subject) — see Pending Decision below
  • FUTURE Equipment line item / billing changes — covered by WO-2, EQ-0

Decided

✓ DECIDED — One subject equipment per WO (1:1)

A WO has exactly one subject piece of equipment. If a tech services 4 rooftop units in one building, that becomes 4 separate WOs.

Reasoning: The whole point of equipment service history (the "Work Orders" tab on Equipment detail) is being able to look at a piece of equipment and see what was done to it. With many-to-many, a WO's notes / hours / materials / signatures would all be ambiguous across the equipment it touched — you couldn't tell which equipment a particular detail was for. 1:1 keeps history meaningful: "this WO is the record of work performed on this RTU on Mar 12." Multi-asset visits become multiple WOs (which the dispatch board / templates make cheap).

Source: Emma, 2026-05-05 PM call — "I think a WO would be distinct to a piece of equipment and if they were to do 4 in a building they would have 4 distinct WOs."

Pending Business Decisions

1. Single FK vs polymorphic subject?

This spec proposes a single FK subjectEquipmentId (Equipment-only). Polymorphism (subjectReferenceModel + subjectReferenceId) would let a WO point at any model — Equipment today, Location or Vehicle tomorrow. The codebase has the polymorphic pattern (Notes, MaintenanceThreshold).

The only future candidate raised in conversation was hypothetical. Recommendation: ship as a single FK; accept the migration cost if a real polymorphic case shows up.

4. Data Model

4.1 Job — add subjectEquipmentId

// Added to existing Job model:
subjectEquipmentId  String?     @db.VarChar(21)
SubjectEquipment    Equipment?  @relation("JobsAsSubjectEquipment",
                                          fields: [subjectEquipmentId],
                                          references: [id])

@@index([subjectEquipmentId], map: "Job_subjectEquipmentId_fkey")

4.2 Equipment — add inverse relation

// Added to existing Equipment model:
JobsAsSubject  Job[]  @relation("JobsAsSubjectEquipment")

4.3 Migration

ALTER TABLE `Job`
    ADD COLUMN `subjectEquipmentId` VARCHAR(21) NULL,
    ADD INDEX `Job_subjectEquipmentId_fkey` (`subjectEquipmentId`),
    ADD CONSTRAINT `Job_subjectEquipmentId_fkey`
        FOREIGN KEY (`subjectEquipmentId`) REFERENCES `Equipment` (`id`)
        ON DELETE SET NULL ON UPDATE CASCADE;
Why on Job, not WorkOrder: per WO-1, a WO is just a Job with jobClassification = WorkOrder. There is no separate WorkOrder model. Adding the FK to Job keeps options open for future job classifications without retrofitting; the V1 UI gates the field to WOs only.

7. Acceptance Criteria

  1. A user creating a WO can optionally select a piece of equipment as the subject; saving persists subjectEquipmentId on the Job row.
  2. A user editing a WO can change or clear the subject equipment.
  3. The WO detail page shows the subject equipment as a chip linked to the equipment detail page.
  4. The WO list page shows a "Subject Equipment" column that is sortable and filterable.
  5. The Equipment detail page (desktop) shows a "Work Orders" tab listing every WO where this equipment is the subject, in reverse-chronological order.
  6. The Equipment detail page (mobile) shows the same Work Orders tab in a mobile list layout.
  7. Tapping a WO row in the equipment Work Orders tab navigates to that WO's detail page.
  8. The subject equipment field is hidden on Job types whose jobClassification is not WorkOrder.
  9. Deleting an Equipment record sets subjectEquipmentId to NULL on referencing Jobs (does not cascade-delete).
  10. Existing line-item-based equipment associations (WorkOrderEquipmentItem, EquipmentTimeSheetRecordValue) are unaffected and remain visible in their existing places.
