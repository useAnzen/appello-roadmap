Trade Level Override on WO Labor Line

A WO labor line normally inherits the user's UserTradeLevel for cost & billable rates. But when a Foreman covers a Journeyman shift on a fixed-bid WO, you need to override the trade level for THAT line so the bill is correct. Per-line override, audited, gated by job.financialView.

Status: Concept Effort: +1-2d on WO-2/WO-3 Extends: LEM-1 · WO-3 Master ref: #16

Permission: existing job.financialView permission (verified pattern via apps/frontend/src/lib/permissionHelpers.ts) gates the override pencil — same gate used by WOX-11

The user's default trade level lives on UserTradeLevel (verified — exists in schema). For each labor line the system resolves: tradeLevelOverrideId ?? user.UserTradeLevel.tradeLevelId. Cost & billable rates flow from the resolved trade level through TradeLevelRate tables. The override only changes the resolved trade level for that one line — does not touch the user's record or any other lines.

3. Key decisions for Emma + Corey to discuss

D1 Who can override?
Recommended: Tie to job.financialView per the master plan's pricing-permission decision (item #17 / WOX-11). Same gate that controls visibility of cost/billable columns also controls who can override the trade level that drives them. Post-V1, can split out via the dedicated workOrder.overrideTradeLevel flag.
D2 Reason text required?
Recommended: Yes — short free-text. Required reason creates accountability and makes the audit log useful when reviewing fixed-bid margins post-mortem. Empty reasons turn into "why is this $400 less than expected?" 6 months later with no answer.
D3 Override fixed at line creation or editable later?
Recommended: Editable until line is invoiced/locked. Once the WO closes and rolls into an invoice, the override locks (just like the rest of the line). Pre-invoice corrections are common; post-invoice corrections require a credit memo — different workflow.
D4 Placeholder labor lines (WOX-05) — does override survive resolution to actual?
Recommended: No — the actual user's trade level wins on resolve. Flag mismatch with a yellow chip ("Placeholder was Plumber · Resolved to Journeyman — review rate"). Allows dispatcher to re-override post-resolve if needed, with a fresh audit entry.
D5 Audit trail visibility?
Recommended: WO detail "Audit" tab (per-WO log) + filterable in admin reports (cross-WO override report, monthly cadence). The audit row records: who, when, from-trade, to-trade, reason, and the line's user/date for context.

4. Roadmap context

WOX-10 sits at the intersection of LEM-1 (labor billable rates) and WO-3 (WO create/edit). Per-line override is the simplest, lowest-risk way to handle the "Foreman covers a Journeyman shift" billing problem without inventing a new rate-overlay system. Pairs cleanly with WOX-11 (pricing visibility) — same permission gates both.
