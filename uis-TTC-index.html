The Time-to-Cash story, in five steps

Today, a Mak Surveying admin spends ~5 hours per month manually copying timesheet hours into invoices, downloading PDFs, and emailing them out. The Time-to-Cash track collapses that into a 2-minute click path. Each phase below has a UIS that turns one manual step into one automatic step.

New · the Time-to-Cash track

NEW

Five proposed specs that, together, take the journey from "approved hours" to "paid invoice" from manual to one-click. TTC-1 is the first and the foundation; the rest layer on top.

TTC-1
Labor Bill Generator (one-click invoice from timesheets) Adds a "Generate Labor Bill" button to the Invoicing tab. Picks a date range, groups by user / trade level / role, builds a Simple invoice automatically.
DESIGN TTC-2
Send Invoice via Email (one-click + PDF attach) Replaces the "download → attach to Outlook" dance. One Send button, contact picker, attached PDF, Mailgun audit trail. Closes Q21030-7709.
DESIGN TTC-3
Invoice Cover Letter / Transmittal Templates A new DocumentTemplate kind for invoice transmittal letters with merge tags (invoice #, amount, period, contact). Auto-attaches to TTC-2 sends.
DESIGN TTC-4
A/R Reminder Engine (auto-nudge unpaid invoices) Configurable reminder schedule per customer (+7 / +14 / +30 past due). Reuses TTC-2 send pipeline + TTC-3 templates. Logs every reminder per bill.
DESIGN TTC-5
LEM Combined Report (cost vs billable, single page) One report: labor / equipment / material rows × cost / billable / margin columns, drillable. Closes Q21030-7740 and Thomas's canonical ask.
DESIGN
