4 Decisions Already Locked (for the record)

Decisions made during the planning conversation that diverge from Corey's WOX track. Each is baked into the relevant Path B spec; flagged here so reviewers see the divergence consciously.

  • Equipment subject cardinality is 1:1, not M:M — corrects WOX-04's misattribution of Emma's position; rationale is service-history meaningfulness (multi-asset visits become multiple WOs).
  • Forms-on-WO scope is "trimmed library", not "per-user-assignment filter" — clarification vs WOX-06; the per-user mobile filter is deferred.
  • Templates are untyped — any active template applies to any WO regardless of jobType. (Considered typed; rejected as unnecessary friction.)
  • Per-customer default template dropped — workflow is "open form → Load template" picker; auto-loading on customer select adds little.
  • Materials reference generic Material only at template tier — specific variant + price selected at runtime by tech (insulation-fitting case: 1" vs 2" depending on pipe size).
  • Labor / Equipment plans on templates are dual-mode — structured (FK) = advisory; free-form (description-only) = creates WorkOrderLaborItem / WorkOrderEquipmentItem on apply.
  • Photo workflow uses AssetReference polymorphic join, not direct FK — naturally supports multiple photos per item; locked down on metadata (no captions, no annotations).
  • Checklist ticked-items-not-deletable — audit invariant; untick first to remove.
  • Apply-on-create only, never apply-on-edit; one template per WO; snapshot semantics.
