Labor Bill Generator

One-click invoicing from approved timesheets. Pick a date range, pick how to group the lines, hit Generate. The system pulls hours from timesheets, prices from billable rates, and produces an editable Simple invoice — same flyout, same workflow, just no copy-paste.

Customer driver: Mak Surveying (and 4+ others) Time saved: ~5 hrs/month per admin Effort: ~2 weeks · 1 engineer

1. Why this matters

Customers who bill labor by the hour (surveyors, mechanical service techs, T&M trades) currently re-enter timesheet data into invoices by hand, every billing cycle, every job. The data is already in the system — Appello just doesn't connect approved hours to the invoice form.

"I have to open every timesheet, write down the hours, then open every invoice and type them in. For 30 jobs at month-end, it's a full afternoon. We just need a button that says 'bill April.'" — Mary, Mak Surveying (May 2026 demo)

The same pain came up in calls with RR Mechanical, Owens Insulation, and Vanos. The fix is small in scope but high in leverage: it removes the most frequently-clicked manual workflow in the app.

2. Who this is for

  • Billing admins at trade-services companies — they're the ones doing the manual data entry today.
  • Office managers / owners — they care that invoices go out on time, because slow invoices = slow cash.
  • Project managers — they review the invoice before it's sent to make sure the right hours are on the right job.
  • (Indirectly) field crew — every accurate timesheet entry now turns into a billable line, so logging time matters more.

3. User stories

Five stories, in standard "As a / I want / So that" form. These are the unit of work for QA.

As a billing admin
I want to generate one invoice for all approved labor on a job for a date range I pick, so that I don't have to copy hours into a blank Simple invoice line by line.
As a billing admin
I want to choose how lines are grouped (by user, by trade level, by role, or one-line summary), so that the invoice matches what my customer expects to see.
As a billing admin
I want to preview the lines and totals before I commit, so that I can cancel and adjust the period if something looks wrong.
As a project manager
I want generated invoices to be fully editable like any Simple invoice, so that I can tweak descriptions, add other lines, or override a rate without fighting the system.
As a billing admin
I want a warning when timesheet entries are missing a billable rate, so that I never send an invoice with a $0 line by accident.

4. A day in the life: today vs. tomorrow

Mary, a billing admin at a 12-person survey shop, is invoicing 30 jobs on the last day of April.

Today (no TTC-1)

  1. Open Job → Invoicing tab → click + Invoice
  2. Pick "Simple" template, type customer + invoice date
  3. Open the timesheet log in another tab, sum April hours per crew member by hand
  4. Look up each user's billable rate from a spreadsheet (or guess)
  5. Type each line: description, hours, rate, tax
  6. Save, download PDF, attach to email
  7. Repeat for the other 29 jobs
~10 minutes per job · ~5 hours/month total

Tomorrow (with TTC-1)

  1. Open Job → Invoicing tab → click Generate Labor Bill
  2. Modal opens with April pre-filled, "One per User" selected
  3. Preview shows the 3 line items with hours and rates already filled
  4. Click Generate; the bill flyout opens, fully editable
  5. Adjust if needed, hit Save
  6. Repeat for the other 29 jobs
~30 seconds per job · ~15 min/month total

6. What's in / out of scope

✓ In scope (v1)

  • "Generate Labor Bill" button on the Job's Invoicing tab
  • Modal with period range, grouping radio, live preview, generate button
  • 4 grouping modes: per user · per trade level · per role · one total
  • Pulls only approved labor TimeSheetDetailRecords on the selected job in the date range
  • Reads sell rate from TradeLevelRate.billableValue with fallback (see Decisions)
  • Creates a Simple-template JobBill with line items linked to source timesheet entries
  • Re-generation prevention: timesheet entries already linked to a non-deleted bill are skipped
  • Empty result handling: clear "no approved hours in this period" message
  • "From timesheet" tooltip on generated lines in the bill flyout

✗ Out of scope (later)

  • Equipment lines on the same bill (TTC-4 / UIS-15B)
  • Material lines on the same bill (LEM-3 + future TTC)
  • Sending the bill by email (TTC-2)
  • Scheduled / recurring auto-generation (TTC-3 will reuse TTC-1 logic)
  • Customer-facing portal (TTC-5)
  • Cross-job billing (one invoice covering 5 jobs at once)
  • Editing rates inside the generation modal — go fix LEM-1 instead

7. Decisions needed

D1 · What happens when a TradeLevelRate has no billable rate?
Three options: (a) reject and force the admin to fix it; (b) silently use cost as the sell price; (c) use cost × Job.defaultLaborMarkupPercent with a warning. Recommend (c) — it's forgiving but visible.
D2 · Default period: last calendar month, last week, or pay period?
Recommend last calendar month (most common billing cadence). User can change.
D3 · Should "Generate Labor Bill" be permission-gated separately from "+ Invoice"?
Recommend reusing the existing jobBill.create permission — same outcome, just less typing.
D4 · Release line: append to 1.33 or open a new Time-to-Cash phase?
Recommend a new "Time-to-Cash" phase under 1.33 since it's a coherent narrative across TTC-1..5; opens a clean PR boundary too.

8. Acceptance criteria (the QA list)

  1. "Generate Labor Bill" button appears on Job → Invoicing tab for users with the existing create permission.
  2. Clicking it opens a modal with period start/end (defaulting to last calendar month), grouping radio, and a live preview.
  3. Changing period or grouping refreshes the preview within 1 second on a 500-line timesheet job.
  4. Preview only includes approved (not draft, not rejected) timesheet entries on this Job in the date range.
  5. Each grouping mode produces the expected number of lines (one per user / per trade level / per role / total).
  6. Lines with no billable rate display a yellow warning banner and use the fallback rate per Decision D1.
  7. "Generate" closes the modal and opens the bill in the standard Bill flyout.
  8. The generated bill behaves identically to a manual Simple bill — edit, save, delete, send all work.
  9. Each generated line has a "from timesheet" tooltip listing the source timesheet entry IDs.
  10. Re-running generation for the same period skips timesheet entries already linked to an existing non-deleted bill.
  11. If no approved hours exist in the range, the modal shows an empty state instead of generating an empty bill.
  12. Deleting a generated bill releases the timesheet entries so they can be re-billed.

Engineering notes (for the implementer, not the reviewer)

Everything below is reference material for the build. The plan doc has the step-by-step.

Data model change

One new join model. No changes to JobBill or JobBillLineItem — the link is one-way from the join, so generated bills look identical to manual bills.

model JobBillLineItemTimeSheetSource {
  id                       String                 @id @db.VarChar(21)
  isDeleted                Boolean                @default(false)
  isArchived               Boolean                @default(false)
  createdAt                DateTime               @default(now())
  updatedAt                DateTime               @updatedAt

  jobBillLineItemId        String                 @db.VarChar(21)
  JobBillLineItem          JobBillLineItem        @relation(fields: [jobBillLineItemId], references: [id], onDelete: Cascade)

  timeSheetDetailRecordId  String                 @db.VarChar(21)
  TimeSheetDetailRecord    TimeSheetDetailRecord  @relation(fields: [timeSheetDetailRecordId], references: [id])

  secondsContributed       Int
  rateApplied              Decimal                @db.Decimal(10, 2)
  rateLabelId              String?                @db.VarChar(21)
  EmployeeAgreementRateLabel EmployeeAgreementRateLabel? @relation(fields: [rateLabelId], references: [id])

  @@unique([jobBillLineItemId, timeSheetDetailRecordId])
  @@index([timeSheetDetailRecordId])
}

API surface

OpTypePurpose
laborBillPreviewQueryReturns lines + totals for the modal preview without writing anything.
createLaborBillFromJobMutationSame inputs as preview; creates the JobBill and source links transactionally.
JobBillLineItem.SourceTimeSheetEntriesFieldNew nested resolver so the frontend can render the "from timesheet" tooltip.

Model methods (in apps/api/src/models/JobBill.ts)

  • previewLaborBillFromJob(input, userId) — read-only; runs the same query/aggregation as create but returns rows.
  • createLaborBillFromJob(input, userId) — wraps the same logic in a transaction, calls BaseModel.createItem for the JobBill, then bulk-creates LineItems and TimeSheetSource rows.
  • resolveLaborSellRate(tradeLevelRateId, jobId, tx) — central rate-lookup helper with the Decision D1 fallback.

Frontend touch points

  • BillingTab.tsx — add the "Generate Labor Bill" button to the same toolbar as "+ Invoice."
  • LaborBillModal.tsx (new) — the modal component, uses useModal + useLazyQuery(laborBillPreview).
  • BillDetails.tsx — add a "from timesheet" badge on line items that have SourceTimeSheetEntries.length > 0.

Permissions / safety

  • Reuses jobBill.create — no new permission flag.
  • The model method validates the user can read the timesheet entries via the standard TimeSheetDetailRecord.getList path (no raw Prisma in the resolver).
  • Preview is read-only and idempotent; safe to re-call on every modal change.

Testing surface

  • Unit: resolveLaborSellRate for all three fallback branches.
  • Unit: each grouping mode produces the expected line count for a fixture timesheet.
  • Integration: re-generating skips already-linked entries; deleting a bill releases them.
  • E2E: full flow from button click to bill flyout open, on a seeded job with 3 users.
