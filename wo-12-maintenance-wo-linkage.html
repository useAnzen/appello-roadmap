Maintenance ↔ Work Order Linkage

Links a planned MaintenanceSchedule to the Work Order that fulfills it. Internal service: WO drives the completion data. External service: existing denormalized fields stay authoritative. Adds a unified service-history view on the equipment page combining both paths.

1. Business Case

Maintenance and work orders describe the same real-world event from two angles. MaintenanceSchedule (UIS-15G) is the plan: this asset is due for an oil change next Tuesday. A Work Order (WO-11) is the delivery: a tech showed up, did the work, captured time and materials, got a customer signature. Today these two systems are disconnected — a completed MaintenanceSchedule stores technicianName as a string and cost as a Decimal, with no link back to the WO that produced those numbers.

That works for external service ("we send the equipment to a third-party mechanic") because there is no internal WO to point at — denormalized fields are the only record. It does not work for internal service, where the WO already contains accurate, structured time, labor, materials, and signatures. Without a link, the office double-enters data and reports cannot reconcile planned vs delivered work.

This spec adds a single optional FK — MaintenanceSchedule.workOrderId — and the workflow glue around it: linking, auto-completion on WO approval, reverse "create WO from this scheduled maintenance" entry, and a unified service-history view on the equipment page that merges both paths.

2. Scope

Already Built

  • DONE MaintenanceSchedule model with status workflow, projection from MaintenanceThreshold, multi-visit chaining, denormalized completion fields (UIS-15G)
  • DONE Job.subjectEquipmentId — the foundational equipment-WO relationship (WO-11)
  • DONE WO status workflow including Approved terminal state (WO-1)
  • DONE Equipment detail page Work Orders tab (WO-11)

In Scope (WO-12)

  • IN Optional FK workOrderId on MaintenanceSchedule (Job FK; nullable)
  • IN "Link a Work Order" action on a scheduled maintenance — picks an existing WO whose subjectEquipmentId matches the maintenance equipment
  • IN "Create Work Order" action on a scheduled maintenance — pre-fills new WO with subject equipment and (optionally) a WO Template based on the maintenance type
  • IN Auto-completion: when a linked WO transitions to Approved, the MaintenanceSchedule auto-flips to COMPLETE with completedDate, secondsAtService, and cost derived from the WO instead of denormalized strings. technicianName is left null on internally-fulfilled rows.
  • IN Reverse impact: when a linked WO is reopened, voided, or has its subjectEquipmentId changed, the MaintenanceSchedule auto-reverts to SCHEDULED (with the originally-projected date or today, whichever is later) — see Pending Decision below
  • IN Service history merge: Equipment detail page exposes a unified Service History tab combining (a) WOs where this equipment is subject and (b) MaintenanceSchedule rows in COMPLETE status with no linked WO (external service)
  • IN Read-only "Linked Work Order" chip on the MaintenanceSchedule detail UI; clicking navigates to the WO
  • IN Read-only "Linked Maintenance" chip on the WO detail UI when one exists

Out of Scope

  • OUT Removing or deprecating MaintenanceSchedule.technicianName / cost / secondsAtService / metersAtService — they remain authoritative for external service rows
  • OUT Changes to maintenance threshold projection logic (UIS-15G) — projection still runs on completion regardless of how completion happened
  • OUT Many-to-one (many MaintenanceSchedules linked to one WO) — see Pending Decision below
  • FUTURE Per-asset reports (TTR, MTBF, lifetime cost) — enabled by this spec, delivered later
  • FUTURE Auto-suggesting a MaintenanceSchedule to link when creating a WO whose subject equipment has overdue scheduled maintenance

Pending Business Decisions

1. What happens when a linked WO is reopened, voided, or its subject equipment changes?

The proposed behavior: auto-revert MaintenanceSchedule to SCHEDULED. Pros: maintenance status reflects reality. Cons: an accidental WO edit could spuriously un-complete a maintenance event. Alternative: keep the schedule COMPLETE but break the link, surface a warning.

Action: Decide before implementation; impacts the WO transition guard.

2. One WO per scheduled maintenance, or many?

This spec assumes 1:1 — one MaintenanceSchedule has at most one WO; one WO can fulfill at most one MaintenanceSchedule. Real world counter-example: a tech may handle two due maintenance events for the same equipment in a single visit (Oil Change + Filter Replacement → one WO). Today UIS-15G handles this via parent/child schedules; we'd need a many-to-one to cleanly model it.

Action: Confirm 1:1 is acceptable for v1, or model as many-to-one (multiple MaintenanceSchedules pointing at the same workOrderId) from the start. Many-to-one costs nothing structurally — it's just removing a uniqueness constraint.

3. Should "Create Work Order from MaintenanceSchedule" use a WO Template?

If WO-6 (Work Order Templates) ships in 1.33, the natural mapping is: maintenanceType on MaintenanceSchedule → a default template ID configured in PropertyValue settings. Pre-fills checklist, forms, materials, labor. Without WO Templates, the create action just pre-fills subject equipment.

Action: Confirm dependency on WO-6 or independence.

Flow — Internal completion path

1
Office sees scheduled maintenance MS-882 (Annual Inspection on RTU-3) is due. Clicks Create Work Order.
2
A new WO is opened with subjectEquipmentId pre-filled to RTU-3 and (if WO-6 ships) the template for this maintenance type pre-loaded. Office assigns to Bob and saves. MaintenanceSchedule.workOrderId is set on save.
3
Bob completes the WO in the field, gets sign-off. Office reviews and transitions WO to Approved.
4
On WO approval, MS-882 auto-flips to Complete. completedDate, secondsAtService, and cost are derived from the WO. UIS-15G's projection logic fires and creates the next instance.

Flow — External completion path (unchanged)

1
Office marks MS-883 (Refrigerant Recharge on RTU-3) as Complete directly, fills in technicianName = "Cool Solutions Inc.", cost = $1,180.00. workOrderId stays null.
2
Service History tab shows this row tagged External, sourced from the denormalized fields.

                                 fields: [workOrderId],
                                 references: [id],
                                 onDelete: SetNull)

@@index([workOrderId], map: "MaintSched_workOrder_idx")

4.2 Job — add inverse relation

// Added to existing Job model:
MaintenanceSchedules  MaintenanceSchedule[]  @relation("MaintenanceSchedulesByWorkOrder")

4.3 Migration

ALTER TABLE `MaintenanceSchedule`
    ADD COLUMN `workOrderId` VARCHAR(21) NULL,
    ADD INDEX `MaintSched_workOrder_idx` (`workOrderId`),
    ADD CONSTRAINT `MaintSched_workOrder_fkey`
        FOREIGN KEY (`workOrderId`) REFERENCES `Job` (`id`)
        ON DELETE SET NULL ON UPDATE CASCADE;

4.4 Computed completion fields (not stored)

When workOrderId is set, the GraphQL resolver returns derived values for the completion fields instead of the stored values:

completedDate    = WorkOrder.approvedAt ?? stored value
secondsAtService = SUM(WorkOrderLaborItem.seconds) for this WO ?? stored value
cost             = SUM(WorkOrderLaborItem + MaterialItem + EquipmentItem totals) ?? stored value
technicianName   = NULL when workOrderId is set; otherwise the stored value

The stored columns are not overwritten when the WO is linked — they remain the source of truth for external service rows. Algorithm precedence: linked WO data wins; stored fields are the fallback.

5. API Surface

5.1 GraphQL Type Extensions

type MaintenanceSchedule {
  # ...existing fields
  workOrderId: String
  workOrder: Job
  # completion fields become resolver-computed when workOrderId is set:
  completedDate: Date
  secondsAtService: Int
  cost: Decimal
  technicianName: String
  # source of truth — what produced the completion data:
  completionSource: MaintenanceCompletionSource!  # "INTERNAL_WO" | "EXTERNAL" | "PENDING"
}

type Job {
  # ...existing fields
  maintenanceSchedules: [MaintenanceSchedule!]!
}

5.2 Mutations

# Link an existing WO whose subjectEquipmentId matches this maintenance's equipment.
linkWorkOrderToMaintenanceSchedule(
  maintenanceScheduleId: String!,
  workOrderId: String!
): MaintenanceSchedule!

# Spawn a new WO pre-filled with subject equipment + (optional) template based on
# the maintenance type. Returns the newly-created WO. The MaintenanceSchedule's
# workOrderId is set in the same transaction.
createWorkOrderFromMaintenanceSchedule(
  maintenanceScheduleId: String!,
  workOrderTemplateId: String  # optional override; otherwise resolved from maintenanceType
): Job!

# Sever the link without changing WO state. MaintenanceSchedule may revert to
# SCHEDULED depending on the resolution chosen for Pending Decision #1.
unlinkWorkOrderFromMaintenanceSchedule(
  maintenanceScheduleId: String!
): MaintenanceSchedule!

5.3 Hooks (existing mutations extended)

  • editJob — when a Job's status transitions to Approved or its subjectEquipmentId changes, fire post-edit hook to sync linked MaintenanceSchedules.
  • editJob — when a previously-Approved WO transitions out of Approved, fire post-edit hook to revert linked MaintenanceSchedules per Pending Decision #1 resolution.

5.4 Service History Query

# Returns merged Service History rows: WOs (subject = this equipment) + external
# MaintenanceSchedule completions (no linked WO). Sorted by date desc.
equipmentServiceHistory(
  equipmentId: String!,
  take: Int,
  skip: Int
): [ServiceHistoryEntry!]!

union ServiceHistoryEntry = WorkOrderServiceEntry | ExternalMaintenanceEntry

type WorkOrderServiceEntry {
  date: Date!
  workOrder: Job!
  cost: Decimal
  technicianName: String  # primary user on the WO
}

type ExternalMaintenanceEntry {
  date: Date!
  maintenanceSchedule: MaintenanceSchedule!
  cost: Decimal
  technicianName: String
}

7. Acceptance Criteria

  1. A scheduled maintenance row can be linked to an existing WO whose subject equipment matches the maintenance's equipment; non-matching WOs are not selectable.
  2. "Create Work Order" from a scheduled maintenance produces a new WO pre-filled with the correct subject equipment and (when WO-6 is present) the appropriate template, and atomically sets workOrderId on the maintenance row.
  3. When a linked WO transitions to Approved, the MaintenanceSchedule auto-flips to Complete and UIS-15G projects the next instance.
  4. While a WO is linked, MaintenanceSchedule's completion fields (completedDate, secondsAtService, cost) reflect the WO's data, not the stored values.
  5. An external completion (no workOrderId) continues to use the stored denormalized fields and is displayed identically to today.
  6. Unlinking a WO from a MaintenanceSchedule reverts the schedule per Pending Decision #1 (target: SCHEDULED).
  7. The Equipment detail Service History tab shows internal (WO) and external entries merged and sorted by date, with a visible Source tag.
  8. Deleting a WO sets MaintenanceSchedule.workOrderId to NULL and the schedule reverts per Pending Decision #1.
  9. Changing a linked WO's subjectEquipmentId away from the maintenance equipment unlinks it (consistency invariant).
  10. The "Linked Work Order" / "Linked Maintenance" cross-chips render on both detail pages and link through correctly.
