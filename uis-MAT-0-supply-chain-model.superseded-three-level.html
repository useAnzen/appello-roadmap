Supply Chain Foundation — ManufacturerProduct + DistributorPrice

The priced supply-chain layer on top of MAT-10. Adds ManufacturerProduct (a manufacturer’s catalogue entry for a material — SKU, MSRP, packaging) and DistributorPrice (what a distributor charges for that product — unit/list price, discount, lead time), and wires the resulting per-distributor cost into the existing bestAvailableCost() hook that LEM-3a left for it. Establishes “who makes / who sells this material, and for how much.” Foundation for MAT-3 (price book), MAT-5 (supplier PO), MAT-9 (discounts).

1. Business Case

After MAT-10, a Material knows which Companies are its manufacturers / distributors (the lightweight MaterialCompany link). What it still can’t answer: what SKU each manufacturer uses, and what each distributor charges. Today the only material cost in the system is MaterialAttributePrice.value (a single catalogue cost per material/cell) and Material.unitCost (0-axis SKUs) — there is no per-supplier price, no notion of a cheapest source, and nothing for procurement (PO sourcing, price-book import) to build on.

MAT-0 adds the two-level priced model the rest of the supply chain depends on:

  • ManufacturerProduct — a manufacturer’s catalogue entry for a material: their SKU, MSRP, UPC, pack size. One Material has many (one per manufacturer that makes it).
  • DistributorPrice — what a distributor charges for a given ManufacturerProduct: unit price, list price, negotiated discount, lead time, min qty. One ManufacturerProduct has many (one per distributor that sells it).
The pricing payoff is concrete and already wired for. LEM-3a’s MaterialPricingService.bestAvailableCost() carries an explicit comment: “MAT-0 will upgrade this to prefer DistributorPrice.effectivePrice for the material’s preferred ManufacturerProduct before falling through to MaterialAttributePrice. Service signature must not change.” MAT-0 fulfils that hook — real distributor cost flows into every sell-price the app already computes, with zero change to LEM-3a’s callers.

2. Scope

The priced relationship + its read/write surfaces + the cost-tier integration. Two new models, the effectivePrice computation and best-cost selection, the bestAvailableCost() upgrade, Company & Material UI to view/manage it, GraphQL + MCP, and the Shopify catalogue-readiness fields that belong with the catalogue. Bulk import, quantity-tier discounts, and PO creation are explicitly downstream.

In Scope

  • IN ManufacturerProduct + DistributorPrice models (+ relations on Material & Company), keeping MaterialCompany (MAT-10) in sync
  • IN effectivePrice (computed) + best-cost selection across a material’s active distributor prices
  • IN Wiring the distributor cost tier into bestAvailableCost() (the MAT-0 hook) — signature unchanged
  • IN Company detail: Products tab (manufacturers) / Products & Pricing tab (distributors), shown by join-existence (not company type)
  • IN Material detail: Supply Chain section (manufacturers + distributor pricing with a best-price marker)
  • IN GraphQL types/inputs/queries/mutations + MCP tools (thin → model)
  • IN Catalogue-readiness fields on Material — sku, barcode, weight, weightUnit (standalone value now; Shopify-forward later)

Out of Scope

  • OUT Bulk price-book import (CSV/XLSX) — MAT-3
  • OUT Quantity/tier discounts & cascading discount resolution — MAT-9 (MAT-0 has a single price + optional flat discountPercent only)
  • OUT Creating Supplier POs from pricing — MAT-5
  • OUT Per-cell distributor pricing for dimensional (X/Y/Z) materials — their per-cell cost stays in MaterialAttributePrice (see §5)
  • OUT isPublishedToShopify / any Shopify sync — deferred until the integration is real (per Shopify forward-compat decision)
  • OUT Product images — already handled by the existing AssetReference polymorphic join (referenceTable = "Material"); no schema change needed

3. Current State — Load-Bearing Givens

Grounded against Release-v1.33.0.

1. bestAvailableCost() is the named MAT-0 hook

apps/api/src/services/MaterialPricingService.ts (LEM-3a, shipped in 1.33). bestAvailableCost(materialId) today: for a material whose group has pricing axes, returns the most-recently-updated MaterialAttributePrice.value; for 0-axis materials, returns Material.unitCost. The file’s header comment reserves the upgrade for MAT-0 and states the signature must not change. resolveMaterialSellPrice() calls it only when the caller doesn’t already supply a costPrice.

Important boundary (from the same comment): for dimensional materials with multiple priced cells, bestAvailableCost is an any-cell fallback — WO/IE structured line items resolve the specific (X,Y[,Z]) cell themselves and pass costPrice in. So MAT-0’s distributor-cost tier is the cost source for 0-axis SKUs and any-cell estimates, not a per-cell override for dimensional price sheets. This is the natural fit — procured supply-chain items are predominantly 0-axis SKUs.

2. MaterialAttributePrice is the existing cost grid — the fallback below MAT-0

schema.prisma ~line 2906: per-material cost keyed on (materialId, selectedXValueId, selectedYValueId[, selectedZValueId]), value Decimal(10,2). Distributor cost sits above this in the resolution order; MAP remains the fallback when a material has no distributor price.

3. MAT-10 MaterialCompany is the prerequisite — MAT-0 keeps it in sync

MAT-10 establishes MaterialCompany(materialId, companyId, role) (the lightweight “this company manufactures/distributes this material” link, rendered comma-joined on the material). MAT-0 layers SKU/price detail on top. Rule: creating a ManufacturerProduct(material, manufacturer) ensures a MaterialCompany(material, manufacturer, Manufacturer) row; creating a DistributorPrice (whose ManufacturerProduct resolves a material) ensures a MaterialCompany(material, distributor, Distributor) row. The manufacturers/distributors MAT-10 displays are thus populated as a side-effect of MAT-0 data entry.

4. Company type is NOT load-bearing (inherited from MAT-10 Q2)

MAT-10 decided company type (a configurable PropertyValue) is ignored for correctness — many tenants only have “Vendor”/“Customer”. MAT-0 honours that: do not gate manufacturer/distributor behaviour on a “Manufacturer” company type. A company shows the Products tab because it has ManufacturerProduct/DistributorPrice rows (join-existence), not because of a type tag. (The CEO draft’s “seed a Manufacturer company type” step becomes optional cosmetic — see Q-f.)

5. Company detail is tabbed; Material has no sku/barcode/weight yet

The Company detail page composes a flat allTabOptions array (apps/frontend/.../CompanyDetailsPage) — a new tab is one entry + one component. Material currently has no sku, barcode, weight, or weightUnit (confirmed greenfield); MaterialGroup.unitOfMeasure exists but isn’t normalized.

4. Data Model Changes

Two plain relational models (not DataModel entities — fixed shape, no user-configurable attributes; same call as MAT-10’s MaterialCompany). IDs are app-generated 21-char (@id @db.VarChar(21), no @default(cuid()) — matches the codebase convention; the CEO draft’s cuid() is wrong for this repo).

model ManufacturerProduct {
    id              String   @id @db.VarChar(21)
    isArchived      Boolean  @default(false)
    isDeleted       Boolean  @default(false)
    createdAt       DateTime @default(now())
    updatedAt       DateTime @default(now()) @updatedAt

    materialId      String   @db.VarChar(21)
    manufacturerId  String   @db.VarChar(21)         // Company (the manufacturer)
    manufacturerSku String   @db.VarChar(100)
    msrp            Decimal? @db.Decimal(10, 2)
    upc             String?  @db.VarChar(50)
    description     String?  @db.Text
    packageQty      Decimal? @db.Decimal(10, 2)
    packageUnit     String?  @db.VarChar(50)
    isActive        Boolean  @default(true)

    Material        Material @relation(fields: [materialId], references: [id])
    Manufacturer    Company  @relation("ManufacturerProducts", fields: [manufacturerId], references: [id])
    DistributorPrices DistributorPrice[]

    @@unique([materialId, manufacturerId, manufacturerSku])
    @@index([materialId])
    @@index([manufacturerId])
}

model DistributorPrice {
    id                    String   @id @db.VarChar(21)
    isArchived            Boolean  @default(false)
    isDeleted             Boolean  @default(false)
    createdAt             DateTime @default(now())
    updatedAt             DateTime @default(now()) @updatedAt

    manufacturerProductId String   @db.VarChar(21)
    distributorId         String   @db.VarChar(21)   // Company (the distributor)
    distributorSku        String   @db.VarChar(100)
    unitPrice             Decimal  @db.Decimal(10, 2)
    listPrice             Decimal? @db.Decimal(10, 2)
    discountPercent       Decimal? @db.Decimal(5, 2)
    effectiveFrom         DateTime?
    effectiveTo           DateTime?
    minQty                Int?
    leadTimeDays          Int?
    isPreferred           Boolean  @default(false)
    isInStock             Boolean?
    notes                 String?  @db.Text

    ManufacturerProduct   ManufacturerProduct @relation(fields: [manufacturerProductId], references: [id])
    Distributor           Company             @relation("DistributorPrices", fields: [distributorId], references: [id])

    @@unique([manufacturerProductId, distributorId, distributorSku])
    @@index([manufacturerProductId])
    @@index([distributorId])
}

// Material: Shopify-forward catalogue-readiness (standalone value now)
model Material {
    // ...existing...
    sku         String?  @db.VarChar(64)
    barcode     String?  @db.VarChar(64)   // UPC/EAN/GTIN, house-level
    weight      Decimal? @db.Decimal(10, 3)
    weightUnit  String?  @db.VarChar(8)
    ManufacturerProducts ManufacturerProduct[]
}

// Company: + ManufacturerProducts / DistributorPrices back-relations (named relations above)

Cardinality: Material 1—N ManufacturerProduct N—1 Manufacturer(Company); ManufacturerProduct 1—N DistributorPrice N—1 Distributor(Company). ManufacturerProduct is a horizontal breakdown by manufacturer (e.g. R-30 batts by Owens Corning vs. Johns Manville) — not a per-attribute/variant cell. SKUs differ: Material.sku = the contractor’s house SKU; ManufacturerProduct.manufacturerSku/upc = the maker’s; DistributorPrice.distributorSku = the seller’s.

No new enums. The model uses booleans (isActive, isPreferred, isInStock) and a free-text packageUnit. If packageUnit is later constrained to a fixed set, introduce it via the app’s enum convention (Prisma enum + const-array + GraphQL enum, PascalCase — like ApprovalStatus), not a raw Prisma enum.

5. Pricing Integration (the centerpiece)

effectivePrice (computed on DistributorPrice)

Not stored. effectivePrice = (discountPercent != null && listPrice != null) ? listPrice × (1 − discountPercent/100) : unitPrice. Exposed as a computed GraphQL field; computed in the model layer so MCP can read it too.

Best-cost selection for a material

Across the material’s ManufacturerProducts → their DistributorPrices, filtered to active rows (not deleted/archived; currently within [effectiveFrom, effectiveTo] or open-ended), pick the cost as:

  1. If any active price is isPreferred → its effectivePrice (preferred wins).
  2. Else the lowest effectivePrice among active prices (cheapest source).

(Whether preferred-overrides-lowest, or lowest-always, is Q-a — it intersects MAT-10’s “no primary” decision.)

The bestAvailableCost() upgrade

Insert a tier above today’s logic, signature unchanged:

bestAvailableCost(materialId):
    cost = bestDistributorCost(materialId)      // NEW (MAT-0): active preferred/lowest effectivePrice
    if (cost != null) return cost
    // unchanged LEM-3a fallback:
    if (group has pricing axes) return latest MaterialAttributePrice.value
    return Material.unitCost ?? null
Net effect: every sell price the app already computes (resolveMaterialSellPrice, the resolvedSellPrice TableFeed column, WO/IE/LEM consumers) now rests on real distributor cost where it exists, automatically — no caller changes. Per §3.1 this applies to 0-axis/any-cell costing; dimensional per-cell costs are still resolved by the caller from MaterialAttributePrice.
Keep the SQL mirror in sync. The no-job sell-price tiers are mirrored as SQL in graphql/models/TableFeed/ConfigureColumnsData/material.ts (resolvedSellPrice). If MAT-0 changes the cost basis that column should reflect distributor cost too — the plan must address whether/how the SQL column reads distributor cost (likely a correlated subquery for best effectivePrice).

6. Company & Catalogue-Readiness

Company detail tabs (join-existence, not type)

  • Products tab — for any company with ManufacturerProduct rows: the materials it makes (SKU, MSRP, pack, active).
  • Products & Pricing tab — for any company with DistributorPrice rows: material / manufacturer / SKUs / unit & list price / discount / lead time / preferred.

Both added to the allTabOptions array; visibility keyed on the join existing, consistent with the MAT-10 company-type decision. No dependence on a seeded company type.

Catalogue-readiness fields

sku / barcode / weight / weightUnit on Material earn their place now as standard catalogue attributes (house SKU, scan code, shipping weight) independent of Shopify; they also happen to be exactly what a future Shopify product/variant needs. No isPublishedToShopify (deferred). Images use the existing AssetReference join (referenceTable = "Material") — no schema change.

GraphQL

    id: ID!  materialId: String!  Material: Material
    manufacturerId: String!  Manufacturer: Company
    manufacturerSku: String!  msrp: Float  upc: String
    description: String  packageQty: Float  packageUnit: String  isActive: Boolean!

    id: ID!  manufacturerProductId: String!  ManufacturerProduct: ManufacturerProduct
    distributorId: String!  Distributor: Company  distributorSku: String!

    effectiveFrom: DateTime  effectiveTo: DateTime  minQty: Int  leadTimeDays: Int
    isPreferred: Boolean!  isInStock: Boolean  notes: String

}
extend type Material {
    ManufacturerProducts: [ManufacturerProduct!]!
    bestDistributorCost: Float          # null when no active distributor price
}
extend type Query {
    manufacturerProducts(materialId: ID, manufacturerId: ID): [ManufacturerProduct!]!
    distributorPrices(manufacturerProductId: ID, distributorId: ID): [DistributorPrice!]!
}
extend type Mutation {
    createManufacturerProduct / updateManufacturerProduct / deleteManufacturerProduct
    createDistributorPrice / updateDistributorPrice / deleteDistributorPrice
}

Create/update of either model also upserts the corresponding MaterialCompany row (§3.3). MCP: thin tools (create_manufacturer_product, upsert_distributor_price, …) delegating to the models, per the golden rule.

9. Open Questions / Decisions Needed

Q-a. Best-cost: preferred-wins or always-lowest? pricing

If a DistributorPrice.isPreferred exists, does it set the cost even when another active price is cheaper, or is cost always the lowest active effectivePrice? Note the tension with MAT-10 Q3 (we dropped a “primary” supplier on the lightweight link).

Lean: preferred-wins when set, else lowest. isPreferred here is a per-price procurement choice (distinct from MAT-10’s display question) and is genuinely useful for sourcing. Confirm with the business.

Q-b. Effective-date filtering in best-cost pricing

Best-cost should ignore prices outside [effectiveFrom, effectiveTo] — confirm, and confirm the “as of” date is “now” (not the WO/estimate date) for the LEM cost basis.

Lean: filter to currently-active (now); open-ended (null) bounds always active.

Q-c. DistributorPrice cost applies at material level only (0-axis / any-cell)? scope

Per §3.1, dimensional materials resolve per-cell cost from MaterialAttributePrice and the caller passes costPrice; bestAvailableCost (where MAT-0 hooks) is the 0-axis/any-cell path. Confirm we are NOT trying to attach distributor prices to specific X/Y/Z cells in MAT-0.

Lean: yes — material-level only. Per-cell supplier pricing, if ever needed, is a separate future item.

Q-d. Do sku / barcode belong on Material? catalogue

House SKU/barcode on Material, maker SKU/UPC on ManufacturerProduct, seller SKU on DistributorPrice — confirm the house-level fields are wanted now (they’re the Shopify-forward set) vs. deferring to whenever Shopify lands.

Lean: add them now — standard catalogue fields, low cost, avoids a second migration later (per the Shopify forward-compat decision; isPublishedToShopify stays out).

Q-e. MaterialCompany sync — auto-manage on delete? data

Creating a ManufacturerProduct/DistributorPrice upserts the MaterialCompany link. When the last ManufacturerProduct/DistributorPrice for a (material, company, role) is removed, do we auto-remove the MaterialCompany link, or leave it (it may have been added manually in MAT-10)?

Lean: leave it — don’t delete a link MAT-0 didn’t exclusively own; manual cleanup via the MAT-10 surface. Avoids surprising removals.

Q-f. Seed a “Manufacturer” company type? optional

The CEO draft seeded a Manufacturer company-type PropertyValue + CompanyPayableType (AP/AR false). Per §3.4 nothing keys off it. Keep as optional cosmetic (companies are easier to find when typed), or drop?

Lean: optional, idempotent (INSERT IGNORE on slug company-type-manufacturer), never required by logic. Don’t assume Distributor/Supplier types exist.

11. Acceptance Criteria

  • ManufacturerProduct + DistributorPrice exist with the stated fields, uniques, indexes, Company/Material relations; ids are @db.VarChar(21) app-generated (no cuid()).
  • Creating either model upserts the matching MaterialCompany row (role Manufacturer/Distributor); removal follows Q-e.
  • effectivePrice computes listPrice×(1−discount/100) when discount+list present, else unitPrice; readable from GraphQL and the model.
  • bestAvailableCost() returns the active preferred/lowest distributor effectivePrice when one exists, else falls through to the unchanged MAP/unitCost logic; signature unchanged.
  • Expired/future-dated and archived/deleted prices are excluded from best-cost.
  • resolveMaterialSellPrice and the resolvedSellPrice TableFeed column reflect distributor cost for 0-axis materials with a distributor price.
  • Company detail shows Products / Products & Pricing tabs by join-existence (not company type); Material detail shows the Supply Chain section with a best-price marker.
  • Material carries sku, barcode, weight, weightUnit; editable on the form; no isPublishedToShopify; images via AssetReference.
  • GraphQL + MCP CRUD present for both models, thin → model.
  • No raw Prisma enum added; build + prettier pass.
