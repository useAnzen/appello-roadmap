Receiving + Two-Way Match

Receive deliveries against Supplier POs with partial/complete receipts, two-way match (Ordered vs Received), and back-order tracking. PO status auto-advances through the customizable Partially Received → Received states. The third match leg (Invoiced) and committed-cost roll-up are tracked as separate follow-up slices.

Parent: Release 1.33 Slice: MAT-6 Est: 4–5 days
Reconciled against the live schema (repo-2) + the MAT-5 locked design
  • Status is a PropertyValue, not an enum. MAT-5 locked PO status as a customizable PropertyValue in the purchase-order-status group, driven by Instance anchors. MAT-6 seeds Partially Received + Received into that group and adds two new anchors — it does not introduce enum statuses.
  • Permission reuses MAT-5’s dedicated supplierPurchaseOrder permission (resolver-enforced, gated by env RESTRICT_MATERIAL) — not materialorder.edit.
  • Two-way match only. No supplier-invoice / AP entity exists in the schema today (only customer-facing JobBill), so the “Invoiced” leg has no data source. MAT-6 ships Ordered-vs-Received; the third leg moves to MAT-11 (Supplier Invoices + Three-Way Match).
  • Committed cost deferred. JobFinancialSummaryService has no “committed” concept (forecast + actuals only; material actuals come from expense line items). The roll-up moves to MAT-12 (PO Committed Cost in Job P&L) — consistent with the MAT-5 deferral.
  • Models follow base conventions. Both new models extend BaseModel (inherit id/createdAt/updatedAt/isArchived/isDeleted); receivedQty/backOrderQty are computed at query time, not stored columns.

1 · Business Case & Customer Evidence ▼

Without receiving, there is no proof of delivery, no way to dispute short shipments, and no automated invoice approval. Accountants manually compare every supplier invoice against orders — a process that breaks at scale. Receiving closes the loop between procurement and accounts payable.

Customer Evidence

"Match packing slip / bill of lading against purchase order. Mark full receipt or partial receipt. Handle back-orders." — Product vision (Nov 2025)
"If a vendor has a credit, it starts with receiving — you can't know what's wrong until you confirm what arrived." — Corey Shelson

Value Delivered

📦
Delivery Confirmation
Formal record of what arrived, when, and who received it — evidence for disputes and audits.
📋
Partial Receipts
Receive what arrived, track what’s back-ordered. Multiple receipts per PO until fully received.
🔀
Two-Way Match
Ordered qty vs Received qty per line, with discrepancies flagged. Lays the groundwork for full three-way match once supplier invoices land (MAT-11).
🔄
PO Status Auto-Transition
PO status auto-advances through the customizable Partially Received → Received states (PropertyValues, per-instance) as receipts are logged.

Competitive Landscape

HAS Procore Receiving against PO with partial receipt tracking
HAS Sage 300 Full receiving + AP matching with three-way match
HAS ServiceTitan Inventory receiving workflow
NONE Appello backOrderValue field only — no receiving workflow

2 · Scope ▼

In Scope

  • IN PurchaseOrderReceipt + ReceiptLineItem Prisma models (both extend BaseModel)
  • IN receivedQty / backOrderQty on PurchaseOrderLineItem — computed at query time from receipts (not stored columns)
  • IN Receiving form: select PO, enter received (and damaged) qty per line item
  • IN Two-way match view on PO detail: Ordered vs Received + back-order, with status indicators
  • IN Two new purchase-order-status PropertyValues (Partially Received, Received) + two new Instance anchors; PO status auto-advances via the anchors
  • IN Optional revised delivery ETA per line when receiving a partial shipment — updates the line item's expectedDeliveryDate (MAT-5) so the back-ordered remainder carries a real date instead of a free-text note. Completes the actual-received leg of the delivery-planning dates
  • IN Receipt history tab on PO detail page
  • IN Receiving gated by the MAT-5 supplierPurchaseOrder permission (+ env RESTRICT_MATERIAL)

Out of Scope

  • OUT Three-way match / Invoiced leg — no supplier-invoice entity exists yet → MAT-11
  • OUT Committed material costs in Job Financial Summary → MAT-12 (net-new “committed” concept in JobFinancialSummaryService)
  • OUT Mobile receiving
  • OUT Barcode/QR scanning on receipt
  • OUT Automatic inventory creation (MAT-7)

4 · Data Model ▼

Both models extend BaseModel — id, createdAt, updatedAt, isArchived, and isDeleted are inherited from the base and are shown below for clarity, not redeclared.

PurchaseOrderReceipt

model PurchaseOrderReceipt {
  id                String    @id @db.VarChar(21)
  isDeleted         Boolean   @default(false)
  isArchived        Boolean   @default(false)
  createdAt         DateTime  @default(now())
  updatedAt         DateTime  @default(now()) @updatedAt
  receiptNumber     String    @db.VarChar(50)   # global RCV-#### sequence (RFI precedent)
  purchaseOrderId   String    @db.VarChar(21)
  PurchaseOrder     PurchaseOrder @relation(fields: [purchaseOrderId], references: [id])
  receivedByUserId  String    @db.VarChar(21)
  ReceivedBy        User      @relation("PurchaseOrderReceiptReceivedBy", fields: [receivedByUserId], references: [id])
  receivedDate      DateTime  @default(now())
  packingSlipNumber String?   @db.VarChar(100)
  notes             String?   @db.Text
  LineItems         ReceiptLineItem[]
  @@index([purchaseOrderId, isDeleted])
}

ReceiptLineItem

model ReceiptLineItem {
  id                      String    @id @db.VarChar(21)
  isDeleted               Boolean   @default(false)
  isArchived              Boolean   @default(false)
  createdAt               DateTime  @default(now())
  updatedAt               DateTime  @default(now()) @updatedAt
  receiptId               String    @db.VarChar(21)
  Receipt                 PurchaseOrderReceipt @relation(fields: [receiptId], references: [id])
  purchaseOrderLineItemId String    @db.VarChar(21)
  PurchaseOrderLineItem   PurchaseOrderLineItem @relation(fields: [purchaseOrderLineItemId], references: [id])
  receivedQty             Decimal   @db.Decimal(10, 2)
  damagedQty              Decimal?  @db.Decimal(10, 2)
  notes                   String?   @db.Text
  @@index([receiptId, isDeleted])
}

Status: PropertyValue group + Instance anchors

MAT-5 made PO status a customizable PropertyValue in the purchase-order-status group (Draft / Issued / Closed / Cancelled), with lifecycle code resolving target states via Instance anchor FKs (the RFI precedent). MAT-6 extends that — no enum statuses:

  • Seed two PropertyValues into the same group: Partially Received (slug purchase-order-status-partially-received, workflowState=inprogress) and Received (slug purchase-order-status-received, workflowState=done).
  • Add two Instance anchor FKs: purchaseOrderPartiallyReceivedStatusId and purchaseOrderReceivedStatusId (mirroring MAT-5’s purchaseOrderDefaultStatusId / ...IssuedStatusId / ...CancelledStatusId).
  • Seed in both the migration (existing instances) and the new-instance bootstrap (future instances), using the CI-safe SQL convention: ids via LEFT(md5(uuid()), 21) inline; resolve each anchor by the unique slug subquery; no SET @var.
UPDATE `Instance` SET
  `purchaseOrderPartiallyReceivedStatusId` =
    (SELECT id FROM `PropertyValue` WHERE slug = 'purchase-order-status-partially-received'),
  `purchaseOrderReceivedStatusId` =
    (SELECT id FROM `PropertyValue` WHERE slug = 'purchase-order-status-received');

PurchaseOrderLineItem — computed accessors (no new columns)

FieldTypeNotes
receivedQtyComputedSum of ReceiptLineItem.receivedQty across all non-deleted receipts for this line
backOrderQtyComputedquantity - Σ(receivedQty − damagedQty) — good (undamaged) quantity still outstanding

Both are computed at query time (GraphQL field resolvers), not stored columns — this avoids drift between receipts and summary fields. Damaged semantics: receivedQty is the total physically delivered; damagedQty is the damaged subset. Only good quantity (receivedQty − damagedQty) counts toward fulfilment, so damaged units keep their line on back-order and feed Vendor Credits (MAT-8).

Queries & Mutations

type Query {
  purchaseOrderReceipts(purchaseOrderId: String!): [PurchaseOrderReceipt!]!
  purchaseOrderReceipt(id: ID!): PurchaseOrderReceipt!
}

type Mutation {
  receivePurchaseOrder(input: ReceivePurchaseOrderInput!): PurchaseOrderReceipt!
}

The receivePurchaseOrder mutation is gated by the MAT-5 supplierPurchaseOrder permission (resolver-enforced, + env RESTRICT_MATERIAL). In a single transaction it: assigns the next RCV-#### number, validates receivedQty ≤ (ordered − previouslyReceived) per line, creates the receipt + line items, then recomputes line totals and sets the PO’s statusId by resolving the Instance anchor — purchaseOrderReceivedStatusId when every line’s good qty meets ordered, else purchaseOrderPartiallyReceivedStatusId. Target states are never matched by label. When a line supplies expectedDeliveryDate, the mutation also updates that line item’s expectedDeliveryDate so the back-ordered remainder carries a live ETA.

The receipt receivedDate is the actual received date — the fourth of the delivery-planning dates introduced in MAT-5 (planned order → actual order → planned received → actual received). Together these let contractors track material availability for manpower planning; surfacing them on the schedule / calendar is a deferred follow-up.

7 · Acceptance Criteria ▼

  • A full quantity can be received against a Supplier PO in a single receipt
  • Partial quantities can be received, leaving a back-order balance on remaining lines
  • Back-order quantity is correctly computed as ordered − Σ(receivedQty − damagedQty) (damaged units stay on back-order)
  • PO status auto-transitions to the Partially Received PropertyValue (via purchaseOrderPartiallyReceivedStatusId) when some but not all good qty is received
  • PO status auto-transitions to the Received PropertyValue (via purchaseOrderReceivedStatusId) when all lines reach ordered quantity
  • Both new statuses are editable per instance (label/color/order) like any other purchase-order-status PropertyValue
  • Two-way match view displays ordered, received, and back-order qty per line with status indicators
  • Each receipt is assigned a unique global RCV-#### number
  • Receipt history tab on PO detail shows all receipts with number, date, receiver, packing slip, and notes
  • Permission check: the supplierPurchaseOrder permission (+ env RESTRICT_MATERIAL) is required to receive against a PO — not materialorder.edit
  • Validation: received quantity cannot exceed remaining quantity (ordered − previouslyReceived)
  • Damaged quantity can be recorded per line item during receiving
  • A revised delivery ETA can be recorded per line when receiving a partial shipment; it updates the line item's expectedDeliveryDate so the back-ordered remainder carries a real date
